I am a PhD researcher at CRAAM, Universidade Presbiteriana Mackenzie. My work combines atmospheric electricity, environmental observations, and scientific computing, with a focus on turning complex geophysical data into reproducible research and useful public tools.

## Platforms I Founded

- **[OpenMonitor](https://openmonitor.org)** — An open-science platform comprising Solar, PG, Journals, and Peers, with interactive tools for exploring scientific observations, literature, and research communities.
- **[Chambica](https://chambica.com)** — An Android-first local-services platform that helps people in Ica find and contact professionals and tradespeople through search, profiles, reviews, and in-app chat.
- **[Aireica](https://aireica.com)** — Live environmental monitoring for Ica, combining air quality, atmospheric potential gradient, meteorology, and public alerts.
